FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How much does it cost to work with you?

Management starts at $1,250/mo. Most clients land on Growth ($2,500/mo). Ad spend goes directly to Meta/Google — we never mark it up.

What is the minimum ad budget?

$1,500/mo for one channel, $3,000+/mo for two. Below that, data comes too slowly to optimise. We'll tell you honestly on the free call if you're not ready.

How fast will I see results?

Early signals in 2–3 weeks, stable learnings by day 60, scaling in months 2–3. Anyone promising overnight ROAS is selling hope, not media buying.

Do you require long contracts?

No. A 90-day sprint to prove the model, then month-to-month. Most clients stay 12+ months because growth compounds.

What reporting do I get?

A live dashboard plus a 5-minute weekly video: what we spent, what it made, what we test next. Monthly strategy call included.

Who owns the ad accounts and creative?

You do. Accounts live in your Business Manager, creative files are yours forever — even if we part ways.
