FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How much does it cost to work with you?

Management starts at ₦100,000/mo ($500 abroad) on Starter. Most clients land on Growth at ₦180,000/mo ($900). Ad spend goes directly to Meta/Google — we never mark it up. Founding clients get their first month at ₦75,000 / $400.

What is the minimum ad budget?

₦100,000/mo in Nigeria (about $500/mo abroad) for one channel, ₦200,000/mo ($1,000) for two. Below that, data comes too slowly to optimise. We'll tell you honestly on the free call if you're not ready.

Where are you based — and do you work outside Nigeria?

We're based in Nigeria and work with ecommerce brands in Nigeria, Europe and worldwide — in your time zone, on WhatsApp and email. Your market doesn't change how we work, only which currency you pay in.

What if it doesn't work?

You start with a free audit, so you see the plan before you pay anything. Founding clients are month-to-month from day one — cancel anytime. And your ad accounts and creative stay yours, even if we part ways.

How fast will I see results?

Early signals in 2–3 weeks, stable learnings by day 60, scaling in months 2–3. Anyone promising overnight ROAS is selling hope, not media buying.

Do you require long contracts?

No. Founding clients are month-to-month from day one. After that, a 90-day sprint to prove the model, then month-to-month. We earn the renewal with results, not paperwork.

What reporting do I get?

A live dashboard plus a 5-minute weekly video: what we spent, what it made, what we test next. Monthly strategy call included.

Who owns the ad accounts and creative?

You do. Accounts live in your Business Manager, creative files are yours forever — even if we part ways.
